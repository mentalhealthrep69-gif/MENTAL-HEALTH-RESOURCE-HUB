# ℹ️ About "Breaking the Silence"

> **Breaking the Silence** is an open-access, student-centered emotional well-being initiative designed to provide accessible mental health guidance, practical self-reflection toolkits, and clear crisis support pathways for university students.

---

## 🎯 Mission & Objectives

* **Promote Mental Health Awareness:** Demystify emotional well-being, destigmatize help-seeking behavior, and equip students with foundational mental health knowledge.
* **Provide Actionable Coping Tools:** Deliver structured, self-paced guides and interactive reflection worksheets to help students manage academic stress, burnout, and everyday life pressures.
* **Bridge Crisis Support:** Ensure immediate, friction-free access to campus emergency triage (`0723 241 952`) and national 24/7 crisis hotlines across Kenya.
* **Foster Inclusive Dialogue:** Gather continuous anonymous student feedback to keep repository resources relevant, empathetic, and impactful.

---

## 🌍 Global & Academic Context

* **SDG Alignment:** Built in support of **Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-Being)**, focusing on mental health promotion and crisis intervention within higher education.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use This Repository

1. **Browse Folders `01` to `03`** for self-guided learning, coping strategies, and reflection worksheets.
2. **Access Folder `04_Local directory`** immediately if you or a peer need urgent crisis intervention or campus medical triage contacts.
3. **Use Folder `05_Feedback`** to share anonymous suggestions or request specific mental health topics.
