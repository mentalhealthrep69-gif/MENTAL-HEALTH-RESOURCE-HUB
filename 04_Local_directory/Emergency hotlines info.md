# 🚨 Mental Health & Emergency Support Directory

> **If you or someone you know is in immediate physical danger or experiencing a life-threatening mental health crisis, please reach out using the contacts below immediately.**

---

## 🏛️ University Emergency Contacts

For immediate on-campus crisis response, counseling services, or urgent welfare assistance:

* **Campus Medical & Counseling Emergency Line:** `0723 241 952`
* **Access:** Call or SMS
* **Availability:** Primary contact for immediate university triage and referral

---

## 📞 24/7 National Mental Health Crisis Hotlines

Free, confidential, and immediate support available across Kenya:

* **Kenya Red Cross Mental Health Line:** `1190` or `1199` *(Toll-Free)*
  * **Services:** 24/7 psychological first aid, trauma response, and crisis counseling.
* **Befrienders Kenya (Suicide Prevention Helpline):** `+254 722 178 177`
  * **Services:** Call, SMS, or WhatsApp support for individuals in distress or experiencing suicidal thoughts.
* **National Mental Health Toll-Free Line:** `0800 723 253`
  * **Services:** Ministry of Health mental health support and referral hotline.

---

## 🛡️ Specialized Support Hotlines

### Gender-Based Violence (GBV) & Abuse
* **National GBV Helpline:** `1195` *(Toll-Free, 24/7)*
  * **Services:** Emergency assistance, counseling, and medical/legal referral for GBV survivors.
* **Tu Ongee GBV Helpline:** `0800 720 565` / `0800 720 553`

### Youth & Student Helplines
* **Childline Kenya / Youth Helpline:** `116` or `0800 221 234` *(Toll-Free, 24/7)*
  * **Services:** Confidential counseling and crisis interventions for young adults and teenagers.

---

## 🏥 Hospital Emergency & Referral Services

* **Mathari National Teaching and Referral Hospital (MNTRH):** `0713 699 715` / `0721 336 017`
  * **Services:** Primary national psychiatric facility for acute crisis management.
* **National Emergency Medical Services:** `999` or `112`

---

## 📝 How to Use This Directory

1. **Save Local Numbers:** Add `0723 241 952` and `1190` to your mobile contacts for quick access during emergencies.
2. **Share Responsibly:** Distribute this file or link within peer groups, student communities, and workshop presentations.
3. **Seek Early Support:** You do not need to be in an extreme crisis to call—these services are there to listen and support you early on.
4. **Accompany a Friend:** If a peer is hesitant to make a call alone, offer to sit with them or place the call together.

---

> 🛑 **Disclaimer:** This directory provides contact details for independent emergency and counseling services. For immediate physical safety threats on campus, contact campus security or local authorities.
