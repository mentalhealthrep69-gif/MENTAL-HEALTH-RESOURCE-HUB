## 🚨 Step 6: Immediate Professional & Emergency Contacts

If your distress continues, worsens, or you feel unable to keep yourself safe, reach out immediately:

* **Campus Emergency Triage Line:** `0723 241 952`
* **Kenya Red Cross Mental Health Line (Toll-Free 24/7):** `1190` or `1199`
* **Befrienders Kenya Helpline:** `+254 722 178 177`
* **National GBV Helpline (Toll-Free 24/7):** `1195`
