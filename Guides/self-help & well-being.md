## 01_Stress_Management_Guide

**Goal:** Handle everyday stress before you feel completely burnt out.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Know Your Triggers:** Notice what makes you feel stressed (like close deadlines or noisy rooms).
2. **Take a 5-Minute Break:** Step away from your work. Relax your shoulders, unclamp your hands, and take 3 deep breaths.
3. **Pick 1 or 2 Tasks:** Choose only 1 or 2 important tasks to finish today instead of trying to do everything.
4. **Move Your Body:** Go for a short 10-minute walk or stretch to release physical tension.
5. **Talk to Someone:** Share your feelings with a friend, mentor, or peer advocate instead of keeping them inside.

> **Remember:** Stress is temporary. Taking short pauses keeps you strong.

## 02_Coping_with_Academic_Pressure

**Goal:** Do well in school without damaging your health.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Set Small Daily Goals:** Study a little bit every day instead of staying up all night to cram.
2. **Separate Study and Rest:** Keep your books and laptop off your bed so your brain knows when it is time to sleep.
3. **Take Frequent Breaks:** Study for 25 minutes, then take a mandatory 5-minute break.
4. **Learn from Mistakes:** View bad grades as a chance to improve your study plan—not as a failure of who you are.
5. **Ask for Help Early:** Talk to your teachers or classmates as soon as you get confused.

> **Remember:** Your grades show how well a study plan worked, not your worth as a person.

## 03_Healthy_Sleep_Guide

**Goal:** Sleep better so you can focus easily and feel good.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Wake Up at the Same Time:** Try to get out of bed at the same time every morning.
2. **Put Screens Away:** Turn off phones, laptops, and TVs 30 to 60 minutes before going to bed.
3. **Keep Your Room Quiet:** Make sure your sleeping area is dark, quiet, and comfortable.
4. **Avoid Late Coffee:** Do not drink coffee, tea, or energy drinks in the late afternoon.
5. **Relax Before Bed:** Read a light book, stretch, or listen to soft music to help your body feel sleepy.

> **Remember:** Sleep helps your brain remember everything you studied during the day.

## 04_Dealing_with_Loneliness

**Goal:** Feel less alone and build good friendships on campus.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Accept Your Feelings:** Remind yourself that feeling lonely happens to everyone at times.
2. **Start Small:** Say hello to a classmate, thank campus workers, or send a friendly text to someone.
3. **Study Around Others:** Move your study spot to the library, student lounge, or cafeteria instead of staying alone in your room.
4. **Join One Campus Activity:** Attend one meeting for a student club, sports team, or campus event.
5. **Be Kind to Yourself:** Spend your alone time doing things you truly enjoy.

> **Remember:** Small conversations build big connections over time.

## 05_Managing_Exam_Anxiety

**Goal:** Stay calm and clear-headed before and during exams.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Test Yourself Early:** Practice explaining study topics out loud without looking at your notes.
2. **Breathe Slowly:** Take slow, deep breaths when you feel nervous or scared.
3. **Change Your Thoughts:** Tell yourself, *"My fast heart rate means my body is getting ready to help me focus."*
4. **Start Easy:** Answer the easiest questions first on your exam paper to build your confidence.
5. **Rest After the Exam:** Do not spend hours arguing over answers with friends after the test is done.

> **Remember:** Calm your body first, and your mind will follow.

## 06_Building_Healthy_Routines

**Goal:** Build simple daily habits that stick without feeling difficult.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Connect Habits Together:** Add a new habit right after an old one (e.g., *"Right after I brush my teeth, I will drink a glass of water"*).
2. **Start Very Small:** Begin with just 2 minutes of a habit (like reading just 1 page of a book).
3. **Protect Your Morning:** Do not check notifications or class messages for the first 15 minutes after waking up.
4. **Track Your Progress:** Cross off completed habits on a paper calendar or notebook.
5. **Get Back on Track:** If you miss a day, do not worry—just try your best to do it again the next day.

> **Remember:** Small actions done every day work much better than big actions done once in a while.

## 07_Grounding_and_Breathing_Techniques

**Goal:** Calm your body quickly when you feel panicked or scared.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Use the 5-4-3-2-1 Rule:**
   * Look for **5** things you can see.
   * Touch **4** things around you.
   * Listen for **3** sounds.
   * Smell **2** things.
   * Taste **1** thing.
2. **Try Two Quick Inhales:** Take two fast breaths in through your nose, then one long slow breath out through your mouth.
3. **Practice Box Breathing:** Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, breathe out for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds.
4. **Relax Your Muscles:** Squeeze your shoulders up for 5 seconds, then let them drop completely.
5. **Feel the Floor:** Press your feet firmly onto the ground and notice how it supports you.

> **Remember:** You cannot argue with a panicked mind. Calm your physical body first.

## 08_Digital_and_Social_Media_Well-being

**Goal:** Protect your peace of mind and time from too much screen use.

### Steps to Follow
1. **Clean Your Feed:** Unfollow or mute social media pages that make you feel bad about yourself.
2. **Keep Screens Away:** Put your phone away while eating meals and while lying in bed.
3. **Turn Off Alerts:** Mute app notifications so your phone does not buzz constantly.
4. **Set a Time Limit:** Use a phone timer to limit social media scrolling to 20 or 30 minutes a day.
5. **Take Real Rest Breaks:** When you feel tired, take a short walk or listen to music instead of scrolling on your phone.

> **Remember:** Your time and attention are valuable. Choose carefully where you spend them online.
