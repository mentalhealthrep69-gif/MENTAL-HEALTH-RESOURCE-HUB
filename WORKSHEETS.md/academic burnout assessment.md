# 🎓 Academic Burnout & Workload Self-Assessment

Academic burnout occurs when chronic study stress leads to physical, emotional, and mental exhaustion. This self-assessment is designed to help you pause, identify early signs of burnout, and reflect on practical ways to protect your well-being.

---

## 📖 Self-Guided Assessment Questions

If you prefer to reflect in your journal or offline, you can walk through the questions below:

### 1. Energy & Capacity Check
* **Mental Fatigue:** On a scale of 1 to 5 (1 = Refreshed, 5 = Exhausted), how mentally drained do you feel at the start of your study day?
* **Workload Balance:** On a scale of 1 to 5 (1 = Overwhelming, 5 = Manageable), how does your current academic load feel?
* **Motivation:** How engaged do you feel with your coursework this week?
* **Emotional Distance:** How often do you feel detached or cynical about your academic goals? *(Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Frequently / Almost Always)*

---

### 2. Burnout Symptoms & Indicators
Check all the signs you have experienced over the past two weeks:

- [ ] Difficulty concentrating or persistent "brain fog"
- [ ] Procrastinating out of dread rather than laziness
- [ ] Sleep disruptions (insomnia, trouble staying asleep, or oversleeping)
- [ ] Physical fatigue, headaches, or muscle tension
- [ ] Feeling overwhelmed by small, everyday tasks
- [ ] Irritability or low patience with peers and family
- [ ] Feeling like no matter how much you study, it isn't enough

---

### 3. Identifying Stress Drivers
What factors are contributing most to your academic pressure right now?

- [ ] Approaching exams, continuous assessment tests (CATs), or major assignments
- [ ] Difficulty keeping up with reading materials or lecture content
- [ ] High personal or external expectations to achieve specific grades
- [ ] Poor study-life balance or lack of leisure time
- [ ] Fear of failure or feeling like an imposter
- [ ] Personal, financial, or health concerns

---

### 4. Self-Compassion & Recovery Action Plan

1. **One Small Boundary:** What is one realistic boundary you can set this week to protect your rest time? *(e.g., stopping all study by 9:00 PM, taking Sunday mornings completely off, turning off notifications during meals)*
2. **Support Needed:** What form of support would help lighten your load right now?
   - Practical time management / study technique guides
   - Connecting with a peer study group
   - Speaking with a university counselor or mentor
   - Dedicated rest without feeling guilty

---

### 🆘 Support & Resources
If academic stress is significantly impacting your daily functioning, health, or sleep, please consider reaching out to campus counseling services or a trusted peer mentor.
