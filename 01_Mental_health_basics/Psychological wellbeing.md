# Psychological Well-Being

While emotional well-being is about *feelings*, psychological well-being is about *how your mind functions* in daily life—your mindset, growth, and sense of purpose.

---

### Core Parts

* **Self-Acceptance:** Liking who you are and accepting your strengths and weaknesses.
* **Purpose:** Feeling like your time, efforts, and goals matter.
* **Independence:** Making your own choices rather than just following what everyone else does.
* **Good Relationships:** Having people in your life who respect and support you.
* **Daily Control:** Feeling capable of managing your daily responsibilities and schedule.
