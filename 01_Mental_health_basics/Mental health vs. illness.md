# Mental Health vs. Mental Illness

People often mix these two terms up, but they mean different things. You can have a diagnosed mental illness and still enjoy good mental health day-to-day if you have the right support.

---

### Comparison

| Mental Health | Mental Illness |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What it is:** The general state of your mind, feelings, and social life. | **What it is:** A health condition diagnosed by a medical professional. |
| **Who has it:** Everyone. | **Who has it:** Some people (e.g., Depression, Anxiety Disorders). |
| **How to support it:** Self-care, rest, healthy habits, talking to friends. | **How to support it:** Therapy, medical care, lifestyle changes, medication. |
| **Example:** Feeling overwhelmed during exam week. | **Example:** Being unable to function for weeks due to severe anxiety. |
