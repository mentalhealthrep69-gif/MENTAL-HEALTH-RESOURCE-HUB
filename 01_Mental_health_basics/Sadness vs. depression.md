# Sadness vs. Depression

It is normal to feel down sometimes, but clinical depression is different from normal sadness.

---

### Comparison

| Sadness | Depression |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cause:** Usually linked to a clear reason (e.g., a breakup or bad grade). | **Cause:** Can happen without any obvious trigger. |
| **Duration:** Lasts a short time (hours or a few days) and comes in waves. | **Duration:** Lasts at least 2 weeks continuously. |
| **Impact:** You can still laugh, work, or enjoy a meal at times. | **Impact:** You lose interest in things you usually love (anhedonia). |
| **Self-Worth:** You still feel okay about who you are as a person. | **Self-Worth:** Deep feelings of guilt, hopelessness, or worthlessness. |
