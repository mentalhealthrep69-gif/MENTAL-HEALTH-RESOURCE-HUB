# 🧠 What is Mental Health?

Mental health encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It influences how we think, feel, act, and handle life's daily stresses, relationships, and decision-making. 

Just like physical health, **everyone has mental health**, and it exists on a continuous spectrum that changes throughout our lives.

---

## 🔍 Key Things to Know

* **It changes over time:** Your mental health is not fixed; it moves back and forth along a scale from feeling good to struggling, depending on what is happening in your life.
* **It affects daily choices:** It shapes how you manage stress, talk to friends, make academic decisions, and view yourself.
* **It is more than "not being sick":** Good mental health does not mean you are happy all the time. It means you have the psychological tools and resilience to cope when things get tough.

---

## 🌟 Why Mental Health Matters for Students

As students balancing academics, personal growth, and social responsibilities, maintaining emotional well-being is vital for success. Prioritizing mental health helps to:

1. **Enhance Focus & Learning:** A clear, supported mind improves memory retention, cognitive function, and academic performance.
2. **Build Resilience:** Healthy coping mechanisms help you bounce back from academic setbacks or life pressures.
3. **Foster Healthy Relationships:** Understanding your own emotions enables you to communicate better and support your peers.

---

> **💡 Remember:** Seeking support, taking breaks, and talking about your feelings are signs of strength, not weakness. You don't have to navigate challenges alone.
