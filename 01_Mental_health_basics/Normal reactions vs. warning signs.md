# ⚠️ Normal Reactions vs. Warning Signs

Life brings hard moments, academic pressures, and personal challenges. Knowing the difference between everyday stress and clinical warning signs helps you understand when to practice self-care and when to seek professional support.

---

## 🟢 Normal Reactions
These are typical emotional and physical responses to life stressors, temporary difficulties, or disappointments:

* **Feeling nervous** before an interview, presentation, or exam.
* **Feeling sad or quiet** after a loss, disappointment, or rough day.
* **Wanting a day alone** to rest and recharge after a busy, exhausting week.
* **Temporary drops in sleep or energy** during high-pressure deadlines.

---

## 🔴 Warning Signs (Time to Ask for Support)
These indicate that your emotional well-being is heavily strained and that professional guidance or support is needed:

* **Time:** Feeling deeply down, hopeless, or anxious for **more than 2 consecutive weeks**.
* **Daily Life:** Struggling to perform basic daily activities such as bathing, sleeping properly, eating, or attending classes.
* **Withdrawal:** Completely stopping all contact with friends, family, and supportive peers.
* **Big Shifts:** Extreme mood swings, sudden outbursts of anger, or a complete loss of energy and interest in things you usually enjoy.
* **Safety:** Thinking about hurting yourself or ending your life.

---

## 💡 What to Do If You Notice Warning Signs

1. **Reach Out:** Talk to a trusted friend, family member, mentor, or campus counselor. You do not have to carry heavy feelings alone.
2. **Utilize Local Resources:** Visit your student health clinic or check the local directory for professional support options.
3. **Be Kind to Yourself:** Acknowledge that recognizing you need help is a powerful step toward healing.

---

> **🚨 Emergency Notice:** If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to emergency services or visit the [Local Directory](../04_Local_directory/01_Readme.md) right away.
