# What is Mental Health?

Mental health is about how we think, feel, act, and handle life's ups and downs. Just like physical health, everyone has mental health.

---

### Key Things to Know

* **It changes over time:** Your mental health is not fixed. It moves back and forth along a scale from feeling good to struggling depending on what is happening in your life.
* **It affects daily choices:** It shapes how you manage stress, talk to friends, make decisions, and view yourself.
* **It is more than "not being sick":** Good mental health does not mean you are happy all the time. It means you have the tools to cope when things get tough.
