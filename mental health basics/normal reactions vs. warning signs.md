# Normal Reactions vs. Warning Signs

Life brings hard moments. Knowing the difference between everyday stress and warning signs helps you know when to seek extra help.

---

### Normal Reactions
* Feeling nervous before an interview or exam.
* Feeling sad or quiet after a loss or disappointment.
* Wanting a day alone to rest after a busy week.

---

### Warning Signs (Time to Ask for Support)
* **Time:** Feeling deeply down or anxious for **more than 2 weeks**.
* **Daily Life:** Struggling to bathe, sleep, eat, or attend classes.
* **Withdrawal:** Stopping all contact with friends and family.
* **Big Shifts:** Extreme mood swings, sudden anger, or complete loss of energy.
* **Safety:** Thinking about hurting yourself or ending your life.
