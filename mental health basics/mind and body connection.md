# The Mind-Body Connection

Your mind and body are constantly talking to each other. What happens in your head affects your body, and what happens in your body affects your mind.

---

### How It Works

* **Mind to Body:** Long-term stress can cause physical symptoms like headaches, stomach pain, muscle tightness, and trouble sleeping.
* **Body to Mind:** Lack of sleep, poor food choices, or sitting all day can make you feel anxious, tired, and irritable.
* **Simple Actions Help Both:** Small physical actions—like taking deep breaths, going for a short walk, or sleeping 7-8 hours—instantly help calm your brain down.
