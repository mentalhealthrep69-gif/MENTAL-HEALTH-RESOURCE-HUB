# 💬 "Breaking the Silence" — Feedback & Suggestions

Thank you for visiting **Breaking the Silence**, a student-led initiative dedicated to emotional well-being and mental health support aligned with Sustainable Development Goal 3.

Your voice matters to us! By providing feedback, you help us improve our resources, expand accessibility, and better serve university students and community members.

---

## 📝 Fill Out the Feedback Form

Click the link below to submit your anonymous feedback directly through our Google Form:

👉 **[Submit Your Feedback Here](https://forms.gle/QDrjcfUcUCWEiqNm9)**

---

## 🔒 Confidentiality & Privacy

* **100% Anonymous:** The form does not collect your email address or personal details unless you choose to provide them.
* **Response Time:** Submissions are reviewed regularly to help guide updates to our toolkits and crisis support resources.

---

## 🚨 Need Immediate Assistance?

If you are currently experiencing a mental health crisis or need urgent support:
* **Campus Medical & Emergency Line:** Call or SMS `0723 241 952`
* **National Mental Health Helpline (Toll-Free 24/7):** Call `1190` or `1199`
