# 💚 Breaking the Silence: Emotional Well-Being Repository

> **"Breaking the Silence"** is a student-led initiative aligned with **Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Well-Being)**. This open-access repository provides practical mental health toolkits, reflection worksheets, crisis support contacts, and educational guides for university students and community members.

---

## 🚨 Quick Access & Immediate Support

* 📞 **[Emergency Hotlines & Crisis Contacts](./local%20directory)** — University hotline (`0723 241 952`), 24/7 national hotlines, and specialized support services.
* 📝 **[Anonymous Feedback Form](https://forms.gle/QDrjcfUcUCWEiqNm9)** — Share your thoughts, suggest topics, or evaluate our resources to help us improve.

## 📌 Initiative Highlights & Features

* **Open Access & Self-Paced:** Peer-focused materials engineered for personal reflection and campus workshops.
* **Triage & Emergency Preparedness:** Direct access to university health contacts and nationwide crisis response numbers.
* **Continuous Student Feedback:** Integrated interactive forms to ensure resources remain relevant and impactful.
