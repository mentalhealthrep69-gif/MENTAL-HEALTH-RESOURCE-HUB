# Stress vs. Anxiety

Stress and anxiety feel very similar in your body, but they come from different sources.

---

### The Difference

Problem outside of you]  ---> STRESS  ---> Fades when the problem ends
[Worry inside of you]    ---> ANXIETY ---> Stays even when there is no immediate threat
* **Stress:** Caused by a clear problem (like an upcoming test or money issue). Once the event passes, the stress usually goes away.
* **Anxiety:** A lasting feeling of fear, dread, or worry about what *might* happen. It can stick around even when things are calm.
