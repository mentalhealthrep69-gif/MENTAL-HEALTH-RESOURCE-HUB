# Emotional Well-Being

Emotional well-being is the ability to notice, accept, and manage your feelings without letting them control your actions.

---

### Core Parts

* **Self-Awareness:** Naming how you feel (e.g., "I am feeling frustrated right now") instead of bottling it up.
* **Healthy Expression:** Finding safe ways to express big feelings—like talking, journaling, or exercising.
* **Bouncing Back:** Recovering after bad news, mistakes, or hard days.
* **Finding Balance:** Making time for activities that bring calm, peace, and joy.
